04 / THINGS I LOVE DOING

A LITTLE MORE
ABOUT MY WORLD.

Outside academics, I find happiness in little creative things. Some help me express myself, some help me slow down, and some simply make my day better.

01 — ART & DRAWING

Where it all began.

Art has been a part of my life since childhood. What started as a simple interest gradually became something I genuinely loved. Over the years, I've participated in different art activities and have been fortunate enough to win awards and prizes for my work. For me, art is more than just creating something beautiful. It's a way of expressing thoughts and imagination.

02 — ORIGAMI & PAPER CRAFTS

Finding peace in little folds.

These days, origami has become one of my favourite hobbies. I love transforming simple sheets of paper into something meaningful, especially handmade origami flowers. There's something peaceful about folding paper, taking my time and paying attention to every little detail. Origami has taught me patience and reminds me that beautiful things take time.

03 — MUSIC

My kind of soundtrack.

Music is something I always come back to, whether I'm working, relaxing or simply spending time with myself. I especially enjoy alternative music. My favourite band is The Neighbourhood. I love the atmosphere, sound and emotions that alternative music brings. A good playlist can make an ordinary day feel a little different.

04 — COOKING

A little creativity in the kitchen.

Cooking is another thing I enjoy. I like experimenting with ingredients, trying different recipes and making something from scratch. Just like art and origami, cooking gives me another way to be creative and enjoy the process of making something with my own hands.

DIFFERENT INTERESTS. ONE CREATIVE MIND.

Little things make life interesting.
