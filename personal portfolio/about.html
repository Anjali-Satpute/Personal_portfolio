A GLIMPSE OF ME.

HELLO, I'M ANJALI.

Quiet by nature.
Creative by heart.

I'm Satpute Anjali, a first-year student at D Y Patil International University. I've always been someone who prefers observing and understanding the world around me rather than being the loudest person in the room. Creativity has been a part of my life since childhood. From art to crafts, I have always enjoyed turning simple ideas into something meaningful.

Coming from a defence background, discipline and adaptability have always been important parts of my life. During my 12th grade, I served as the Senior Under Officer (SUO) in NCC, an experience that taught me leadership, responsibility and teamwork.

My journey has taken me through Bhutan, Rajasthan, Punjab and Hyderabad. Having lived in Hyderabad for the past 12 years, I've grown to appreciate the different cultures and experiences that have shaped who I am.

Today, I'm exploring technology, learning web development and finding new ways to combine creativity with it.
